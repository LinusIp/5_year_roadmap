# DDPM, DDIM and classifier-free guidance from scratch

**Goal.** A diffusion model written from the papers: the forward noising process, a U-Net denoiser, DDPM training and sampling on CIFAR-10, then a DDIM sampler and classifier-free guidance on top, with FID reported for each.

**Why.** Inference Engineering roadmap: `inference-engineering/diffusion-models` and `inference-engineering/few-step-image-generation`. Skills: diffusion models, the U-Net, and PyTorch training at a scale that takes hours, not minutes. Every later generative project (latent diffusion, flow matching, the DiT on your engine's frames, the world model) reuses this code, so the noise schedule and the sampler need to be right here.

**Scope.** MVP: linear and cosine noise schedules, epsilon prediction, a 30–40M-parameter U-Net with time embeddings, residual blocks and attention at 16×16, class conditioning with label dropout, an exponential moving average of the weights, ancestral DDPM sampling, a deterministic DDIM sampler, classifier-free guidance, FID with a fixed protocol. Stretch: v-prediction, learned variances from Improved DDPM, mixed precision and `torch.compile` speed-ups.

**Architecture.** A schedule module precomputes the alphas and their products. The trainer samples a timestep and noise, builds the noisy image in closed form, and regresses the noise with the U-Net, dropping the class label 10 % of the time. Samplers share the trained network: DDPM walks all 1,000 steps, DDIM a chosen subsequence, and guidance mixes the conditional and unconditional predictions. An evaluator generates 10,000 samples and computes FID.

```
x0 ─▶ q(xt | x0) ─▶ U-Net(xt, t, y) ─▶ MSE(ε̂, ε) ─▶ EMA weights
                                                       │
noise ─▶ sampler (DDPM / DDIM, guidance w) ◀──────────┘ ─▶ samples ─▶ FID
```

**Stack.** Python 3.12, PyTorch 2.x, torchvision for CIFAR-10, clean-fid, Weights & Biases, one 24 GB GPU for roughly 10 hours of training.

**Milestones.** Forward process and noise schedules with unit tests (4 h) · U-Net with time embedding and class conditioning (7 h) · DDPM training and ancestral sampling on CIFAR-10 (10 h) · DDIM sampler and the quality-against-steps trade-off (5 h) · Classifier-free guidance with samples across guidance scales (5 h) · FID evaluation and write-up (4 h). Total 35 h.

**Done when.** A unit test checks that the closed-form noisy image matches iterating the forward process step by step. DDPM with 1,000 steps reaches FID 20 or better on 10,000 class-conditional samples against the CIFAR-10 training set. DDIM with 50 steps comes within 3 FID of that. A guidance sweep over w in 0, 1, 2, 3, 5 and 7 produces sample grids and an FID for each scale. All numbers are regenerated by one evaluation script from the saved checkpoint.

**Measure and report.** FID against training steps; FID and sampling time against DDIM steps (10, 20, 50, 100, 250) next to DDPM at 1,000; FID against guidance scale, with the visual diversity trade-off described; training throughput in images per second and total GPU-hours.

**Pitfalls.** An off-by-one between the timestep index and the alpha arrays, which produces samples that are almost right. Sampling with the raw weights instead of the moving average. Images normalised to 0–1 in training and treated as −1 to 1 in sampling. FID on too few samples or against the wrong reference set, so numbers swing by several points between runs. Guidance applied without training the unconditional branch through label dropout.

**References.** ddpm, ddim, classifier-free-guidance, improved-ddpm, paper-unet, annotated-diffusion, lilian-weng-diffusion, hf-diffusion-course.

**Write-up.** Sample grids from DDPM, DDIM at 10 and 50 steps, and three guidance scales; the FID-against-steps and FID-against-guidance charts; and a paragraph on why DDIM can skip steps without retraining.
