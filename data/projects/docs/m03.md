# RAG chatbot over your own notes with a local model and a 30-question eval set

**Goal.** An offline question-answering tool over a folder of your Markdown and PDF notes that retrieves, answers with citations, says so when nothing relevant was found, and is scored on a 30-question eval set.

**Why.** It covers the whole retrieval-augmented loop on hardware you own: `ai-engineer/rag`, `ai-engineer/chunking`, `ai-engineer/embedding`, `ai-engineer/open-source-embeddings`, `ai-engineer/vector-database`, `ai-engineer/chroma`, `ai-engineer/semantic-search`, `ai-engineer/retrieval-process`, `ai-engineer/generation` and `ai-engineer/ollama`. The skills are embeddings, vector search, prompting and evaluation; the eval set is the habit that matters most.

**Scope.** MVP: ingest Markdown and PDF, heading-aware chunking, a local embedding model, a Chroma index, top-k retrieval, a local LLM through Ollama or llama.cpp, citations by file and heading, a "not in my notes" path below a similarity threshold, a CLI and the eval script. Stretch: hybrid BM25 plus vector search, a cross-encoder reranker, incremental re-indexing when a file changes, a small web UI.

**Architecture.** The ingester walks the folder, extracts text, splits it into chunks of 300–800 tokens with overlap and metadata (file, heading, page), embeds them and writes them to Chroma. At query time the retriever embeds the question, fetches the top k chunks and drops those under the threshold. The generator builds a prompt with numbered chunks and an instruction to cite them, calls the local model and returns the answer with its sources. The evaluator replays the 30 questions and scores retrieval and answers.

```
notes/ ─▶ ingest ─▶ chunk ─▶ embed ─▶ Chroma
                                        │ top-k
question ─▶ embed ─▶ retrieve ◀─────────┘
                      │ chunks + sources
                      ▼
                  local LLM ─▶ answer [1][3]  or  "not in my notes"
```

**Stack.** Python 3.12, PyMuPDF or pypdf, sentence-transformers (bge-small or all-MiniLM), Chroma, Ollama or llama.cpp serving a 3–8B instruct model at 4-bit, Typer for the CLI.

**Milestones.** Ingestion and chunking with metadata (5 h) · embeddings and Chroma index (4 h) · retrieval, prompt and local generation with citations (7 h) · threshold and refusal path (3 h) · 30-question eval set and scoring script (7 h) · tuning runs and write-up (4 h). Total 30 h.

**Done when.** The whole pipeline runs with networking disabled. Every answer cites at least one chunk by file and heading, and the cited chunk contains the supporting text. Of the 30 questions, at least 5 are deliberately unanswerable from the notes, and at least 4 of those get the refusal. Retrieval hit rate at k = 5 is at least 80 % on the answerable ones, and answer quality is graded on a 0–2 rubric, by hand or by a judge model checked against 10 hand grades.

**Measure and report.** Retrieval hit rate at k = 1, 3 and 5 and mean reciprocal rank; mean answer score and refusal accuracy; the effect of two chunk sizes and of the threshold; end-to-end latency p50 and p95 on your machine; index size and ingest time for the whole folder.

**Pitfalls.** Chunks that cut tables and code blocks in half. Mixing embedding models between ingest and query after an upgrade. Tuning the threshold on the same questions you report on; keep 10 as a dev split. The model citing chunk numbers it was never given. A small context window in the local model silently truncating the retrieved chunks.

**References.** llm-zoomcamp, paper-rag, llama-cpp, hf-llm-course, w-ml-embedding-search, roadmap-ai-engineer.

**Write-up.** A GIF of three questions (one refused), the eval table for each configuration, a chart of hit rate against k, and what a reranker or hybrid search changed or would change.
