# 3D rigid bodies: broadphase, GJK/EPA, stacking and constraints

**Goal.** The 2D engine's ideas in three dimensions: a dynamic BVH broadphase, GJK and EPA for convex shapes, persistent contact manifolds and a sequential-impulse solver that stacks ten boxes and a pyramid of fifty, with sleeping bodies and debug drawing.

**Why.** Game Developer roadmap: `game-developer/gjk`, `game-developer/epa`, `game-developer/dbvt`, `game-developer/broad-phase`, `game-developer/narrow-phase`, `game-developer/convex-hull`, `game-developer/moment-of-inertia` and `game-developer/quaternion`. Skills: GJK and EPA, inertia tensors and contact persistence. This is the rigid-body core of the stage 1 engine; the cloth, the fluid and the robot arm all collide against it.

**Scope.** MVP: boxes, spheres, capsules and convex hulls; quaternion orientation and world-space inverse inertia; a dynamic AABB tree with fat AABBs; GJK for distance and intersection, EPA for penetration depth and normal; contact manifolds of up to four points, reduced and matched across frames; a sequential-impulse solver with friction and warm starting; island-based sleeping; debug drawing. Stretch: a ball-and-socket and a hinge joint; speculative contacts for fast bodies; a SIMD or multithreaded solver.

**Architecture.** Each step the dynamic tree updates moved proxies and reports overlapping pairs. The narrowphase uses analytic tests for sphere and capsule pairs and GJK with EPA for the rest, then builds or refreshes a persistent manifold per pair. Islands of touching bodies are solved together; islands below the motion thresholds for half a second go to sleep. The debug renderer draws contacts, normals and tree nodes in your engine.

```
bodies ─▶ dynamic AABB tree ─▶ pairs ─▶ GJK / EPA ─▶ persistent manifolds
                                                          │
           islands ─▶ impulse solver (warm start) ◀───────┘
               │
          integrate ─▶ sleep check ─▶ debug draw
```

**Stack.** C++20, your OpenGL or Vulkan renderer for display and debug lines, Dear ImGui for controls, doctest or Catch2, Tracy for profiling.

**Milestones.** 3D body state: quaternions, inertia tensors in world space, integrator (5 h) · Dynamic AABB tree broadphase (7 h) · GJK and EPA with unit tests on known configurations (10 h) · Persistent contact manifolds and the 3D sequential-impulse solver (10 h) · Stacking, pyramid, sleeping and debug drawing (8 h). Total 40 h.

**Done when.** GJK distances match analytic values within 1e-5 on 1,000 random sphere, box and capsule pairs, and EPA depths within 1e-4, in unit tests. A stack of ten boxes stays standing for 60 seconds at 60 Hz with 10 iterations, and a pyramid of fifty boxes settles and sleeps within 3 seconds. A sleeping pile wakes when hit. Debug drawing shows contacts, normals and the tree.

**Measure and report.** Step time against body count (100, 500, 2,000); broadphase pairs against true contacts; GJK iteration counts by shape pair; stack drift against solver iterations with and without warm starting; the share of bodies asleep in a settled scene and the step-time saving.

**Pitfalls.** Applying the body-space inertia tensor in world space without rotating it. GJK looping forever on degenerate simplices; cap iterations and handle the touching case. EPA on shapes inflated by a margin without removing it from the depth. Manifold points matched only by position, so warm starting jumps when a box rotates. Sleep thresholds so loose that a slowly tipping stack freezes mid-fall.

**References.** ericson-rtcd, catto-box2d-publications, paper-iterative-dynamics, millington-game-physics, gamemath-3d-primer, m15.
