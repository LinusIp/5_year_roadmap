# Software rasterizer in C++ (tinyrenderer)

**Goal.** A CPU rasterizer in C++ that loads an OBJ model and renders it textured and lit with perspective-correct interpolation, a z-buffer, back-face culling and programmable vertex and fragment stages, at a measured frame rate.

**Why.** Writing the pipeline yourself shows what a GPU does for you. It builds rasterization, barycentric coordinates and C++, and the nodes `game-developer/rasterization`, `game-developer/graphics-pipeline`, `game-developer/shader`, `game-developer/texture`, `game-developer/perspective`, `game-developer/culling`, `game-developer/clipping` and `game-developer/c-cpp`.

**Scope.** MVP: Bresenham lines, triangle filling by bounding box and barycentrics, a z-buffer, model-view-projection and viewport matrices, perspective-correct UVs, back-face culling, an OBJ loader, image output, vertex and fragment shaders as C++ callables, Gouraud and Phong shading, and a window for live frame-rate measurement. Stretch: near-plane clipping, shadow mapping, tangent-space normal maps, ambient occlusion, multithreaded tiles.

**Architecture.** The model supplies positions, UVs and normals. For each triangle the vertex shader returns clip-space positions and varyings; the pipeline culls back faces, divides by w, maps to the viewport and walks the bounding box. For each covered pixel it computes barycentrics, tests depth, interpolates the varyings with perspective correction and calls the fragment shader for the colour.

```
OBJ ─▶ vertex shader ─▶ cull ─▶ ÷w ─▶ viewport ─▶ raster (barycentric)
                                                     │ depth test
               framebuffer ◀── fragment shader ◀─────┘ ─▶ image / window
```

**Stack.** C++20, CMake, stb_image and stb_image_write (or the course's TGA code), SDL2 or minifb for a window, the tinyrenderer head model, perf or Tracy for profiling.

**Milestones.** Lines, wireframe and OBJ loading (5 h) · triangle filling, barycentrics and z-buffer (7 h) · camera matrices and perspective-correct texturing (7 h) · shader interface, Gouraud and Phong shading, culling (8 h) · live window, profiling and one optimisation (5 h) · README and comparison images (3 h). Total 35 h.

**Done when.** The head model renders textured and lit with no holes or double-drawn pixels on shared edges (a top-left fill rule). A checkerboard plane at a steep angle keeps straight lines, which proves the UVs are perspective-correct. Vertex and fragment stages are swappable callables, with at least three shaders in the repository. Frame time at 800×800 is measured and reported for one thread and for several.

**Measure and report.** Frame time p50 and p99 at 800×800 and at 1920×1080; pixels shaded per second; the share of time in rasterization against shading, from a profiler; the speed-up from the one optimisation you made.

**Pitfalls.** Affine instead of perspective-correct interpolation, which warps textures. Cracks or overdraw on shared edges without a consistent fill rule. Precision loss in edge functions for large or thin triangles. The y-axis flipped between image and screen. Triangles crossing the near plane exploding without clipping.

**References.** tinyrenderer, scratchapixel, cmu-15-462, gamemath-3d-primer, w-gfx-line-rasteriser.

**Write-up.** A progression strip (wireframe, flat, Gouraud, textured, Phong), the checkerboard test, the timing table with a profiler flame graph, and what moving to the GPU will change.
