# Daily scenes

Every image in this folder is listed in `scenes.json` with the palette the build drew from it, and here with its
source. Only images the user has the rights to ship: ones they generated or made, or ones under a licence that
allows use in an app. Nothing is downloaded or copied from Pinterest, Instagram or any other site.

The originals live in `scenes-src/` (git-ignored); `npm run scenes` writes the AVIF and WebP files here at 900 px
(never enlarged) and 300 px wide, each under 180 KB, and the palette rows (`scripts/scenes.ts`).
